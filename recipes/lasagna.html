Recipe Name:

LASAGNA

2 servings

Layers of pasta, slow-cooked beef ragù, béchamel, and melted cheese, baked until golden.

Ingredients:

  • 200gr Tomato sauce
  • 100gr Ground beef
  • 200 gr Milk
  • 20gr Butter
  • 20gr All purpose Flour
  • 40gr Lasagna sheets
  • 90gr Parmesan cheese

Procedure:

  1. Prepare the bolognese with the tomato sauce and ground beef
  2. Heat the butter with the flour to create the Roux, once golden add the milk and cook in low fire until you get the desired texture for the bechamel
  3. Cook the lasagna sheet in boiling water fro 2 minutes
  4. Assemble the lasagna with one layer of each and sprinkle parmesan on top
  5. Cook for 4 min in strong oven
Notes:
