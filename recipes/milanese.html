Recipe Name:

CHICKEN MILANESE

1 serving

Breaded chicken tight, golden and crispy on the outside. tender and juicy inside.

Ingredients:

  • 200gr Chicken tight fillet
  • 50gr Panko
  • 1 pc Egg
  • 20gr All purpose Flour
  • 50gr Parmesan cheese

Procedure:

  1. Pass the chicken through the egg and the panko
  2. Fry in a cacerole with oil
  3. Sprinkle parmesan on top
