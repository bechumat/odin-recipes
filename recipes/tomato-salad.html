Recipe Name:

TOMATO SALAD

4 servings

Creamy sweet and sour tomatoes with crunchy breadcrumbs and gazpacho foam

Ingredients:

  • 300gr Tomato
  • 50GR White sugar
  • 50gr Water
  • 2gr Chili oil
  • 10gr Panko
  • 2gr Garlic
  • 2gr Maldon salt
  • 100gr Tomato gazpacho soup
  • 15gr Cucumber

Procedure:

  1. Make a syrup with the water and the sugar
  2. Cut the tomatoes in quarters and marinate them in the syrup
  3. Fry the Panko with the garlic until crispy
  4. Chopp the cucumber into small cubes
  5. Place the gazpacho soup in a co2 siphon and charge
  6. Place the tomatoes with some chili oil and salt in the plate
  7. Sprinkle the cucumber on top
  8. Finish with the Gazpacho foam
Notes:
This recipe uses a co2 siphon with 2 gas charges
