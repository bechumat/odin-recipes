Recipe Name:

NICOISE SALAD

4 servings

Niçoise salad with seared tuna, green beans, potatoes, tomatoes, olives, boiled egg, and a Diion vinaigrette.

Ingredients:

  • 20gr Tomato
  • 10gr Red onion
  • 1 pc Boiled egg
  • 5gr Green olives
  • 100gr Romaine lettuce
  • 30gr Marble potatoes
  • 15gr Green beans
  • 60gr Fresh tuna
  • Dijon vinaigrette

Procedure:

  1. Slice the onions, tomatoes olives and egg
  2. Boil the marble potatoes for 14 minutes
  3. Sear the tuna slice on each side and slice
  4. Mix the greens with the vinaigrette
  5. Add the rest of the Ingredients on top
Notes:

Sear the tuna for little time to have a tataki style modern salad
