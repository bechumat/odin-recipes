Recipe Name:

CHEESECAKE

8 servings

Creamy baked cheesecake on a buttery biscuit base.

Ingredients:

  • 200gr Maria cookies
  • 50 gr Butter
  • 200gr Cream cheese
  • 400gr Whipping Cream
  • 150gr White sugar
  • 6 pcs Egg

Procedure:

  1. Blend the cookies with the soften butter and place in the base of the moulder
  2. Blend the rest of the ingredients together
  3. Place the mix on top and bake for 15 min at 175 degrees Celcius
  4. Let rest for 8 hours before opening
Notes:

Use a 28cm moulder
